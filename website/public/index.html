Recording and uploading are two different decisions.

Local recording does not automatically send your audio anywhere. Cloud AI starts with a separate upload confirmation.

What happens after an upload?

Uploaded audio, transcripts and chat are stored on the backend with no automatic content-expiry period. Signing out or canceling a subscription does not delete cloud data. Account-deletion fulfillment remains under verification.

What does Google sign-in access?

Clerk handles Google sign-in using basic identity, email and profile access, not Calendar or Drive access. Signing in is separate from sending recording content to Gemini.

Always get the necessary permission before recording or uploading other people's information.
